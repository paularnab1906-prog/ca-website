Expert Income Tax Return (ITR 1-7) Filing in Kolkata

Navigating the Income Tax Act requires more than just uploading Form 16. With the introduction of the Annual Information Statement (AIS) and Taxpayer Information Summary (TIS), the Income Tax Department automatically cross-verifies your stock transactions, mutual fund redemptions, dividend income, and high-value credit card spending.

At Abinash & Co., we conduct a comprehensive pre-filing audit of your financial records, ensuring complete alignment between your books, bank accounts, and portal data to prevent Section 143(1) intimation demands or Section 139(9) defective return notices.

Our 4-Step Income Tax Filing Process

1 AIS, TIS & Form 26AS Audit

We download and reconcile your live AIS/TIS data against your bank statements, Demat reports, and Form 16 to catch TDS credits and unreported interest income.

2 Old vs. New Tax Regime Comparison

We evaluate your tax liability under both tax regimes, calculating eligible deductions under Chapter VI-A (Sec 80C, 80D, 80CCD(1B), Sec 24(b) housing loan interest) to select the most beneficial option for you.

3 Capital Gains & Foreign Asset Disclosure

Accurate computation of Short-Term and Long-Term Capital Gains (STCG/LTCG) on real estate, equity shares, and mutual funds, including Schedule FA disclosure for foreign stocks (US equities/ESOPs).

4 Draft Review, E-Filing & Acknowledgement

You receive a detailed tax computation statement for final sign-off before we submit your return on the e-filing portal and complete electronic verification (e-Verification).

📋 Required Document Checklist for ITR Filing

  • Form 16 / Form 16A (TDS Certificates from employer/banks)
  • Bank Account Statements for the entire Financial Year
  • Capital Gains Statement from Broker / Zerodha / Groww / CAMS / Karvy
  • Interest Certificates for Savings, Fixed Deposits & Home Loans
  • Proof of Tax Saving Investments (80C, ELSS, NPS, Health Insurance)
  • Passport details & overseas income statements (for NRI Tax returns)
