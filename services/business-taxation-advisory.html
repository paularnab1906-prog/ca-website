Frequently Asked Questions (Business Taxation)

What is the turnover threshold for Section 44AB Tax Audit?

For businesses, tax audit is mandatory if turnover exceeds ₹1 Crore (threshold increases to ₹10 Crores if cash transactions are within 5%). For professionals, audit applies if gross receipts exceed ₹50 Lakhs (₹75 Lakhs under 44ADA condition).

What are the MCA ROC annual filing deadlines and late fees?

AOC-4 (Financial Statements) must be filed within 30 days of AGM, and MGT-7 (Annual Return) within 60 days of AGM. Late filing attracts an additional fee of ₹100 per day under MCA rules.
