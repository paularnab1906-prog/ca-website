Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common queries about our CA services, documentation, and process.

Which areas and clients do you serve? −
We serve clients across Kolkata, West Bengal, Odisha, and Pan-India globally, combining remote digital compliance with in-person consultation at our head office for all tax, accounting, and corporate filings.
How do I file my income tax return through you? +
Just send us your Form 16, bank statements, investment proofs and any other income details. We compute your tax, suggest eligible deductions, share a summary for your approval, and file the return — then send you the acknowledgement for your records.
Do you handle GST registration and monthly returns? +
Yes. We manage new GST registration, monthly and quarterly returns (GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B), input tax credit reconciliation, and respond to any departmental notices on your behalf so you stay fully compliant.
Can you help register a new Private Limited company or LLP? +
Absolutely. We handle the complete incorporation process — name approval, DSC, DIN, drafting documents and filing with the MCA — and then support you with ongoing ROC and annual compliance.
What documents do I need to bring for the first meeting? +
For individuals: PAN, Aadhaar and any income proofs. For businesses: PAN, registration certificates, bank statements and recent financials. If you are unsure, call us first and we will send you a simple checklist.
How are your fees structured? +
Fees depend on the scope and complexity of the work. We always discuss and agree a clear, fixed quote up front before starting — so there are no surprises later.
