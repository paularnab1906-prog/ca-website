Consequences of Late Filing

  • TDS Returns: Late fee of ₹200 per day under Section 234E, plus interest up to 1.5% per month on delayed payments.
  • GST Returns (GSTR-1/3B): Late fee of ₹50 per day (₹20 for Nil returns), plus 18% p.a. interest on net tax payable.
  • Income Tax (ITR): Late fee up to ₹5,000 under Section 234F, plus 1% per month interest under Section 234A.
  • ROC Filings (AOC-4/MGT-7): Additional fee of ₹100 per day, plus potential prosecution/penalties on directors.
